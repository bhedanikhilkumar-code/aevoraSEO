## 2. Digital PR System
<a id="digital-pr-system"></a>

Digital PR workflow for earning authority links and unlinked brand mentions through credible, editorially valuable assets.

### Angles
- **Founder & Company Story:** Origin, contrarian viewpoint, unique technical challenges overcome.
- **Original Research & Data:** Benchmarks, survey results, proprietary industry metrics.
- **Local Community Impact:** Local hiring, scholarships, charity partnerships, regional events.
- **Industry Commentary:** Rapid reaction to industry news, regulatory changes, or market shifts.
- **Case Studies & Whitepapers:** Detailed implementation teardowns with concrete numbers.
- **Awards & Recognition:** Industry certifications, ethical standards, innovation awards.

### Targets
- Tier-1 and regional news publications.
- Niche industry blogs and trade journals.
- Relevant business and technical podcasts.
- Industry association newsletters and portals.

### Delivery Output
- Curated pitch angle tailored to specific journalist/editor beat.
- Target publication list with named editors.
- Asset outline or embargoed summary.
- Client destination URL (must be high-value asset, never generic sales page).
- Success criteria: editorial link, branded mention, or syndicated reference.

## 3. Backlink Quality & Prospect Scoring
<a id="backlink-quality-scoring"></a>

Use the [AevoraSEO Reputation Score](../../references/reputation.md) for verified existing links and mentions. An unbuilt placement is a prospect, not an observed backlink. Evaluate prospects qualitatively:

| Check | What to Record |
|---|---|
| **Relevance** | Audience, topic, and geographic fit; explicit reason client belongs. |
| **Placement** | Exact URL or submission route, content context, and editorial ownership. |
| **Contribution** | Useful article, case study, data, profile, or real audience benefit. |
| **Target & Anchor** | Relevant client page and natural descriptive or branded anchor text. |
| **Independence** | Independent editorial, directory profile, community, or owned resource. |
| **Commercial Terms** | Explicit price, sponsorship fees, or editorial disclosure requirements. |
| **Evidence Gaps** | Access limits, editorial route, index declarations, or unverified claims. |

**Decision:** Choose `pursue`, `needs review`, or `poor fit` with evidence. Avoid PBNs, link farms, automated directory blasts, and unverified third-party DA claims.

## 4. Anchor Text Strategy
<a id="anchor-text-strategy"></a>

Safe, natural anchor text planning for sustainable authority growth:

### Anchor Categories
- **Brand Anchor:** `AevoraSEO`, `AevoraSEO Academy`
- **Naked URL:** `https://example.com`, `example.com/guide`
- **Partial-Match / Compound:** `SEO audit tool by AevoraSEO`, `AevoraSEO crawl guide`
- **Topical / Descriptive:** `technical SEO audit checklist`, `answer engine optimization guide`
- **Generic / Contextual:** `read the full report`, `documentation`, `source`
- **Exact-Match:** `SEO crawler` (use conservatively and only in high-relevance editorial contexts)

### Core Rules
- Choose anchors from editorial context, not arbitrary mathematical percentage formulas.
- Never repeat identical exact-match anchors across multiple external websites.
- Use branded and URL anchors for directory profiles, citation listings, and free community resources.
- Use natural sentence anchors for guest contributions and press coverage.

## 5. Free Posting Workflow
<a id="free-posting-workflow"></a>

When executing free placement research:
1. **Inspect Target Website:** Identify real audience, core services, and high-value landing pages.
2. **Select Candidates:** Select 15 relevant destinations (or 20 when justified by scope) from `posting-sites.json`.
3. **Tailor Specifications:** Define exact article angle, outline, target URL, permitted placement location, and submission steps.
4. **Platform Adaptation:** Distinguish editorial guest articles, technical tutorials, QA answers, and community discussions.
5. **Verification:** Publish only with authorization; verify live indexing and dofollow status post-launch.

## 6. Competitor Link Gap Analysis
<a id="link-gap-analysis"></a>

Compares backlink authority footprint against direct search competitors:
- Identify overlapping vs unique referring domains across competitors.
- Filter for replicable link categories: local citations, niche directories, association member directories, resource pages, and guest platforms.
- Prioritize replication:
  1. Local and industry associations.
  2. Authoritative industry directories.
  3. Partner/vendor listings.
  4. Curated resource pages.
  5. Relevant editorial guest articles.

## 7. Toxic Link Risk & Disavow Policy
<a id="toxic-link-risk"></a>

### Risk Signals
- Irrelevant niches (e.g. gambling, adult, pharmaceuticals mixed into unrelated B2B sites).
- High-volume automated comment spam or forum signatures.
- Expired-domain PBN footprints (identical IP subnets, matching WHOIS, cloned CMS templates).
- Heavy exact-match anchor spikes from low-quality non-indexed sites.
- Automated scrape/aggregation networks with zero real organic traffic.

### Action Hierarchy
- **Ignore:** Low-value, harmless scraper links that search engines already discount natively.
- **Avoid:** Stop building patterns that mimic low-quality networks.
- **Request Removal:** Direct contact for serious, targeted negative SEO attempts.
- **Disavow:** Reserved strictly for clear manual-action threats or severe manipulative footprints under expert review. Do not routinely disavow harmless low-authority links.

## 8. Backlink Outreach Templates
<a id="backlink-outreach-templates"></a>

### Local Sponsorship / Partnership Outreach
```text
Subject: Community / event sponsorship inquiry

Hi [Name],

We are interested in supporting [event/community/resource]. If you maintain a partner or sponsor page, please share details and requirements.

Website: [URL]
Relevant Page: [Target URL]

Best regards,
[Name]
```

### Editorial / Guest Article Outreach
```text
Subject: Article idea for [Publication/Site]

Hi [Name],

I wanted to suggest a practical article for your readers: [Topic]. It will cover [brief value] and address [audience problem].

If you accept guest contributions, I would be happy to share an outline first.

Best regards,
[Name]
```

### Resource Page Outreach
```text
Subject: Suggested resource for your [Topic] page

Hi [Name],

I came across your resource page on [Topic]. We recently published [Resource/Research], which provides [specific benchmark/data/guide].

Link: [Target URL]

If you feel it would be helpful for your readers, feel free to reference it.

Best regards,
[Name]
```
