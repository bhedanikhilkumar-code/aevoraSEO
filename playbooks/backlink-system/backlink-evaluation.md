# Paid Backlink & Niche Edit Evaluation Playbook

Evaluates paid sponsorships, guest placements, and negotiated niche edits against referral value, brand objectives, search safety, and link spam policies.

Evaluate advertising and sponsorships against transparent business value and verified terms. Paid links must be appropriately qualified (`rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`); never treat a branded anchor as a substitute for mandatory link qualification or promise synthetic ranking credit. See [Google’s link-spam guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).

---

## 1. When to Use
Use when evaluating:
- Direct guest post offers and sponsored review opportunities.
- In-content link insertions (niche edits) into existing aged articles.
- Commercial directory listings, resource page pitches, or partner sponsorships.

---

## 2. Evaluation Criteria & Checks

1. **Topical & Contextual Relevance**: Does the host website and surrounding article closely align with the target brand's industry, audience, and geographical market?
2. **Organic Traffic & Indexing**: Is the hosting page indexed? Does the root domain receive real, organic search impressions or is it a de-indexed PBN?
3. **Outbound Link Pattern**: Does the domain link out promiscuously to unrelated high-risk niches (casino, crypto, pharma, essay mills)?
4. **Editorial Integrity**: Is the content written by genuine human contributors with editorial oversight, or is it low-quality AI-generated spinning?
5. **Anchor Text Fit**: Will the anchor text fit naturally into the sentence syntax, or does it force an unnatural exact-match phrase?
6. **Price & Commercial Fairness**: Is the requested fee justified by verified audience engagement and referral potential rather than third-party vanity metrics?

---

## 3. Standard Verdicts & Actions

- **Buy / Proceed**: High contextual relevance, natural traffic, strict editorial standards, and properly qualified terms.
- **Avoid (High Risk)**: Obvious link farm, unindexed pages, multi-niche commercial outbound linking, or forced exact-match requirements.
- **Negotiate**: High-quality content host asking inflated pricing without traffic justification.
- **Branded / Naked URL Only**: Moderate authority site where commercial keyword anchors carry unnatural link-profile risk.
- **Nofollow / Sponsored Required**: Any commercially sponsored placement or paid partnership.
- **Need More Data**: Metrics unverified, missing URL, or opaque placement details.
