# Full Site Audit & Issue Prioritization Workflow

Start with `playbooks/core/seo-house-workflow.md` for the complete cross-discipline coverage and deliverable contract. This playbook supplies the detailed audit checks, scoring formulas, issue prioritization logic, and delivery standards. The backlink/authority stage must consult the saved source catalog and produce relevant named URLs, not only prospect categories. Entity, authority, and REO/reputation need explicit findings and actions alongside SEO/AEO/GEO.

A full-site audit is not a checklist dump. It is a decision system that identifies what is blocking ranking, visibility, authority, conversion, and AI-search readiness.

---

## 1. Core Evaluation Sequence

Run the full audit in this order:
1. Capability detection & audit mode selection
2. Business model & commercial goal understanding
3. Site discovery & crawlability/indexability review
4. Page inventory & architecture mapping
5. Technical SEO (status codes, canonicals, JS rendering, robots)
6. On-page SEO & heading hierarchy
7. Content quality, E-E-A-T & direct answer readiness
8. Schema & structured data validation
9. Internal linking, orphan recovery & topic clusters
10. Search intent & query cannibalization
11. Competitor gap analysis
12. Local SEO & GBP signals (if relevant)
13. Backlink & authority analysis
14. AEO/GEO answer box & citation readiness
15. Conversion path, friction & CTA analysis
16. Measurement & tracking validation
17. Issue prioritization & 30/60/90-day roadmap

---

## 2. Issue Prioritization Scoring Model

To prevent unprioritized 100-item task lists, rank all findings using the objective 4-factor scoring model:

$$\text{Priority Score} = \text{Impact} + \text{Confidence} - \text{Effort} - \text{Risk}$$

Where each factor is scored from 1 to 5:
- **Impact (1–5)**: Direct effect on indexing, crawlability, core revenue rankings, or conversion.
- **Confidence (1–5)**: Degree of empirical certainty based on captured logs, DOM evidence, or GSC data.
- **Effort (1–5)**: Development time, engineering complexity, or organizational friction.
- **Risk (1–5)**: Chance of negative ranking side effects, broken URLs, or template regression.

### Priority Action Buckets
- **Must Fix Now (Score $\ge 5$ or Critical Blocker)**: Technical crawl/index blockers, sitewide noindex, broken money page forms, wrong canonical targets.
- **High Impact Next (Score 3 to 4)**: High-intent money page optimization, direct answer boxes, primary schema additions, orphan page recovery.
- **Strategic Build (Score 1 to 2)**: New topic cluster expansion, supporting guides, long-term backlink outreach.
- **Monitor (Score 0)**: Moderate heading nesting skips, minor image alt omissions, low-impression query variations.
- **Ignore for Now (Score $< 0$)**: Trivial cosmetic issues, non-impacting micro-optimizations.

## 3. Page Inventory & Technical Checks

For sampled or crawled URLs, inspect and record:
- Status code, indexability signals, canonical target.
- Title tag, meta description, H1 count, heading structure.
- Word count, substantive content vs boilerplate ratio.
- Primary intent (Informational, Transactional, Commercial, Local, Navigational).
- Schema types present vs recommended.
- Inbound and outbound internal links, orphan status.
- Primary CTA, trust signals, friction points.
