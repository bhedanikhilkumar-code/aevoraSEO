# Reputation SEO Proof Stack

Reputation SEO means proof.

Google, AI systems, and humans should see that the person/company/course/service is real, trusted, and supported by external signals.

---

## 1. Reputation Layers

```text
1. Website proof
2. Review platforms
3. Social proof
4. Media / third-party proof
5. Authority / backlink proof
```

---

## 2. Website Proof Pages

Create:

```text
/reviews/
/testimonials/
/student-results/
/client-results/
/case-studies/
/certifications/
/media/
/awards/
/portfolio/
```

## 4. Third-Party Authority Article Rules

A good reputation article:

```text
Talks about the market/problem first
Mentions multiple options fairly
Mentions the brand as one credible example
Links to proof
Does not sound like fake praise
Uses safe anchors
```

Extract on-site proof and links to known review/media pages. Evaluate supplied review exports and dated search observations separately. A linked profile does not verify its contents; inaccessible third-party evidence stays unverified.

# Third-Party Authority Article System

This module teaches AevoraSEO how to create third-party articles that build authority without looking like propaganda.

---

## 1. Core Rule

Do not write:

```text
[Brand] is the best.
```

Write:

```text
An industry problem, trend, comparison, or guide where the brand appears as one credible example.
```

---

## 2. Article Types

```text
Industry trend article
Problem-solution article
Comparison article
Beginner guide
Case-study style article
Expert opinion article
Tool/platform roundup
Local market analysis
Research summary
Founder journey with lessons
```

---

## 3. Article Formula

```text
1. Start with the industry problem.
2. Explain why it matters.
3. Discuss common solutions.
4. Mention neutral examples/categories.
5. Insert the brand/client as one example.
6. Link to proof.
7. End with useful advice.
```

---

## 4. Anchor Text Rules

Use:

```text
brand name
founder/person name
official website
service/category phrase
case study phrase
review/proof page phrase
```

Avoid exact-match anchor spam.

---

## 5. Example

Bad:

```text
AevoraSEO Academy is the best AI academy in Pakistan.
```

Better:

```text
Why Practical AI Education Is Becoming More Important Than Tool Tutorials in Pakistan
```

Then discuss universities, YouTube learning, international courses, local bootcamps, and mention AevoraSEO Academy as one implementation-focused example with student proof.

## 7. Evidence Workflow

Scrape supplied prospect and published-article URLs, then inspect text, link attributes and observed canonical/noindex signals. Discovering every brand mention, measuring authority/traffic and verifying actual indexing require separate evidence. Publishing or contacting prospects is outside a read-only crawl.
