# 30 / 60 / 90-Day SEO Action & Growth Plans

Comprehensive execution framework for phased SEO rollouts across the first 90 days. Moves deterministically from technical foundations and critical blockers into cluster expansion, answer depth, and authority compounding.

---

## 1. Phased Architecture Overview

```text
Month 1 (Days 1–30):  Foundations, Tracking & Critical Blockers
Month 2 (Days 31–60): Content Architecture, Answer Depth & Internal Linking
Month 3 (Days 61–90): Authority Compounding, CTR Refinement & Gap Closure
```

---

## 2. Month 1: 30-Day Foundation Plan

### Purpose & Critical Priority Buckets
The 30-day plan answers what must be fixed immediately to eliminate crawl, index, and conversion blockers, setting up reliable measurement.

1. **Bucket 1: Measurement and Tracking**
   - Google Search Console & GA4 verification.
   - Form submit, phone click, WhatsApp click, and booking event tracking.
   - GBP UTM tagging and conversion goal definitions.

2. **Bucket 2: Technical Blockers**
   - Remove unintentional `noindex` or `robots.txt` disallows on core pages.
   - Fix canonical mismatches, redirect chains, and critical 404/5xx errors.
   - Resolve JavaScript rendering dependencies on essential body content and metadata.

3. **Bucket 3: Existing Winner Optimization**
   - Query positions 4–20: fastest rank improvement wins.
   - High-impression low-CTR pages: title and meta description rewrites.
   - High-traffic low-conversion pages: CTA and trust proof optimization.

4. **Bucket 4: Highest-Value Money Pages**
   - Core service, primary product, and high-intent commercial landing pages.
   - Heading hierarchy remediation (single H1, logical H2/H3 nesting).

5. **Bucket 5: Local & GBP Quick Wins**
   - Complete Google Business Profile categories, hours, photos, and appointment links.
   - Local NAP consistency and initial citation audit.

6. **Bucket 6: Safe Authority Foundations**
   - Clean citation directory listings, partner/vendor profiles, and brand entity references.

## 3. Month 2: 60-Day Expansion Plan

### Purpose & Architecture
The 60-day plan builds on the stabilized technical base by expanding keyword coverage, publishing missing money pages, strengthening topical clusters, and improving internal linking.

1. **Build Missing Money Pages**
   - Service area pages, course specializations, transactional comparison guides.
   - Each page requires unique title/meta, single H1, intent-aligned body, trust proof, FAQs, and schema.

2. **Publish Supporting Topic Content**
   - Informational guides, procedural how-to articles, definition hubs.
   - Strict hub-to-spoke and spoke-to-hub internal linking hierarchy.

3. **Internal Link Optimization**
   - Homepage to tier-1 hub pages.
   - Spoke content linking up to primary money pages with keyword-rich descriptive anchors.
   - Orphan page recovery and crawl depth flattening ($\le 3$ clicks).

4. **AEO & Answer Box Engineering**
   - 40–60 word direct-answer summaries immediately following question headings.
   - Implementation of `FAQPage`, `Article`, and `Service` schema markup.

## 4. Month 3: 90-Day Authority & Optimization Plan

### Purpose & Compounding Growth
The 90-day plan transitions the rollout into ongoing optimization, closing competitive gaps, and compounding authority.

1. **Authority & Link Acquisition**
   - Industry association listings, high-relevance editorial placements, partner roundups.
   - Continued toxic link monitoring and anchor text distribution control.

2. **Performance Refresh & CTR Optimization**
   - Re-auditing low-CTR queries in GSC positions 1–5 for title/meta snippet split-testing.
   - Updating thin or aging pages with refreshed statistics, expanded definitions, and new media.

3. **Conversion & User Journey Friction Testing**
   - Heatmap/form friction review, high-intent CTA button wording adjustments.
   - Guarantee and objection-handling FAQs on all conversion-focused URLs.

## 5. Execution Rules

1. Every task must state **What**, **Why**, **Owner**, **Priority**, and **KPI**.
2. Tasks that do not improve crawlability, indexability, ranking potential, conversion, local visibility, authority, or tracking must be excluded.
3. Never promise guaranteed search rankings or timeline-fixed traffic figures.
