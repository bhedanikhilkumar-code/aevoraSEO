# Competitor Discovery & Crawl Workflow

Systematic workflow for discovering, evaluating, crawling, and benchmarking direct commercial competitors and aspirational industry benchmarks.

---

## 1. Discovery and Qualification Workflow

Use [the shared research workflow](../../docs/discovery-and-competitors.md). Establish the client's actual business before searching: home, About, Contact, services, relevant cases, industries, and served markets. Preserve explicit owner context. Separate physical offices, served markets, website language, and search query language. Flag conflicts and unknowns.

Generate queries from supported services, commercial/customer intent, market, and language. Search snippets are leads only. Inspect a broader candidate pool using native page captures before making the final selection.

Write reviewed natural category/service phrases and customer questions before searching. Reject incompatible services, customer personas, and business models before scoring:
- Shared keywords such as *AI*, *agency*, or *design* do not make two businesses comparable.
- Directories, marketplaces, publishers, software products, and training providers belong to their actual categories; they are not automatically direct agency competitors.

Apply the reviewed profile keys and transparent rubric:
- Services fit: 40 points
- Customer alignment: 25 points
- Target markets: 15 points
- Business model: 10 points
- Language parity: 5 points
- Empirical evidence: 5 points

Report only supported competitors. For each, include exact website and supporting pages, services/markets, why comparable, important differences, confidence, and gaps. Separate direct business competitors, query-specific search competitors, and aspirational benchmarks.

---

## 2. Competitor Crawl and Comparison Standards

Capture a candidate's homepage, About/Contact, and core service pages matching the client's primary offers. Inspect relevant cases and service areas. A search snippet does not prove business identity, services, or markets. Use native HTTP/browser captures, recording capture dates and quality limits.

Compare equivalent offers and customer questions, not arbitrary page sizes or visual polish. Extract actual titles, headings, FAQs, schema, calls to action, implementation details, cases, and attributable third-party reputation evidence. Missing content cannot be inferred from an incomplete capture.

For each proposed improvement, specify:
1. Client's exact target URL.
2. Competitor's supporting page URL.
3. Observed difference in content, schema, or CTA depth.
4. Why it matters to the client's customers.
5. Concrete implementation action and verification check.

A competitor having a feature is not itself a reason to add it. Never infer search authority, traffic volume, conversion rates, rankings, or AI citations from page appearance alone.

Use [the profile and selection contract](../../docs/discovery-and-competitors.md) and [audit delivery standard](../../references/audit-delivery.md).
