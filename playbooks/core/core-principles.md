# Core Principles & Quality Standards

Foundational rules, definitions, and quality standards for every AevoraSEO task.

---

## SEO, AEO, and GEO Definitions

**SEO** improves crawlability, relevance, authority, and conversion for search engines and users.

**AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization) makes content easier for answer engines to extract and use: direct answers, definitions, steps, FAQs, comparisons, costs, and trust signals.

**GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization) improves the chance of being mentioned or cited by generative search systems through entity clarity, source-worthiness, topical authority, and structured content.

AEO/GEO is not a trick. It is strong SEO plus clearer, more trustworthy, answer-ready content.

---

## SEO Principles

SEO recommendations must connect to crawlability, indexability, topical authority, search intent, local visibility, backlinks, conversion, AEO/GEO visibility, or measurement.

Do not rely on keyword stuffing, fixed keyword density, or outdated checklist thinking. Build helpful, crawlable, structured, trusted pages that satisfy real search intent.

Traffic is not the final goal. Qualified leads, calls, bookings, enrollments, sales, authority, and measurable growth are the real outcomes.

---

## Data Quality Rules

Use quality labels: Confirmed, Likely, Directional, Estimated, and Not Verified.

Do not invent search volume, rankings, traffic, backlinks, reputation estimates, GBP calls, or conversions. Record source, date, metric definition and coverage for supplied authority data. A website crawl cannot calculate a complete backlink profile. Use the documented AevoraSEO rubric only for its observed sample.

---

## Decision Tree

If a website URL and local Python runtime are available, use the bundled crawler. Add local rendered comparison when needed. Add supplied data exports for measurements outside website HTML. If network access is unavailable, use supplied files. If no data is available, provide an advisory plan and identify the first useful source to collect.

---

## No-Fluff Rules

Avoid empty advice like "optimize SEO", "write quality content", "build backlinks", or "improve visibility" unless followed by exact actions.

Every recommendation should include: issue, evidence, impact, exact fix, priority, and measurement.

Never guarantee rankings or invent data. Label estimates clearly.

---

## Final Output Standards

### Audit Output

Include what was checked, what was found, why it matters, what to do next, priority, and missing data.

### Strategy Output

Include goal, current situation, blockers, execution plan, KPIs, risks, and next action.

### Backlink Output

Include relevance, risk, authority, traffic, anchor advice, target page, and verdict.
